How it works. Each antenna is solved as real wires over real ground with a method-of-moments solver (the same idea as NEC), trimmed to resonance the way you would trim it in the yard. Feedline loss comes from datasheet curves, including the extra loss SWR causes. Propagation is a simplified sky-wave model: F2-layer hops, MUF from the sun's position and solar activity, D-layer absorption, and man-made noise at each end. Use it to compare setups. Conditions on the day will vary.
